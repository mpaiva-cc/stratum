currently in a six-week falsification evaluation with two design
partners. It has not shipped and is not generally available. The

four scenes walking one consent-bound talent pool through
identity-resolved rediscovery, a consent-native nurture send
that is stopped at the graph edge when consent does not cover
the purpose or jurisdiction, a candidate-facing transparency
surface, and lifecycle-triggered rediscovery with cited reasoning.
A T+68d gate decides — on adoption evidence — whether Ember
becomes a standalone product, a module inside Stratum Recruiter,
or is shelved. That gate has not run. No adoption numbers exist
yet; they are among the things the gate exists to produce.

## 5. Delivery Brief for Helm

### What this quote package commits Stratum to

- That consent is enforced at the edge of the graph — at the
  storage layer, not in application code — and that a send
  without a valid consent edge has no edge to traverse, so it is
  architecturally impossible rather than merely policy-blocked.
  Do not soften this claim; it is the precise architectural
  difference the quote is built on.

- That the tour is a storyboard of a *proposed* product, under
  a six-week evaluation with published gates. The tour shipped;
  the product has not. This line must not blur.

- That Stratum will report the gate outcome publicly, win or lose.
  The build-in-public posture is on record in Briefings 019 and 020.

### The one misreading to pre-empt

The polished tour reads easily as a launch. It is not. If any
reporter writes "Stratum launches Ember" or "Stratum ships a
new CRM product," Helm should correct it on the record, promptly.
The correction is simple: "The tour shipped; the product is
proposed and under evaluation. The gate runs in approximately
six weeks."

### What NOT to say

- Do not name the agent running Ember. The naming review is open.
  If a reporter asks, the answer is: "The agent that runs it is
  not yet named. That review is still open."

- Do not name Mercator or Halcyon as customers. They are design
  partners. The distinction is real and material.

- Do not quote a gate metric as a result. The gates are published
  as targets. None has been measured yet. "We need one hundred
  percent consent coverage on outreach" is fine. "We have one
  hundred percent consent coverage on outreach" is not true.

- Do not give a launch date or a general-availability target.
  Stratum's public GA target is Q1 2027 for shipped products;
  Ember is not yet greenlit as a product line. Ember's own
  timeline is gated on the T+68d decision, which has not run.

- Do not use stratum.ai as the company's web address. The
  project address is https://mpaiva-cc.github.io/stratum.
  A short verbal response in a press call needs no URL.
