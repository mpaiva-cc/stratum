Digital Omnibus is law. Friday's search-index hint verified; the 2 August cliff moves to 2 December 2027.

Fact: the Digital Omnibus on AI published as Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 in the Official Journal of the European Union on 24 July 2026 and entered into force on 27 July 2026 — today, per the standard third-day-after-OJ-publication rule [1][2][3][4][5]. The urgent third-day entry-into-force (rather than the ordinary twenty-day gap) was chosen deliberately: the pre-existing 2 August 2026 general-application date for high-risk AI systems was six days away at OJ-publication moment; the Council needed the amendments in force before that cliff engaged to avoid a five-day legal-uncertainty window. Fact — the deadline map: high-risk Annex III standalone systems (recruitment, credit, biometric categorisation, etc. — the category Stratum ships into) obligations moved from 2 August 2026 to 2 December 2027; Annex I embedded-safety-component systems (machinery, aviation, etc.) to 2 August 2028; national regulatory sandboxes deadline pushed to 2 August 2027; transparent-marking of AI-generated or manipulated content compressed from six to three months (2 December 2026); Article 5 prohibitions, general-purpose AI rules, and Article 50 transparency duties keep their original schedule; Article 5 expanded with new bans on generation of non-consensual intimate imagery and child sexual abuse material [1][2][4]. The Digital Omnibus also amends the Basic Aviation Regulation and Machinery Regulation for cross-sectoral alignment [3]. Fact — Friday's ambiguity resolved: the single search-index title Eglin surfaced from gamingtechlaw.com on Friday and could not verify against primary sources at 14:01Z that day (three direct EUR-Lex / Consilium / digital-strategy fetches all 403) was correct on the publication event; the three legal-press primary sources still restating "awaiting" language on Friday afternoon (Freshfields, DLA Piper GENIE, Gibson Dunn) had not yet refreshed against the Friday-morning OJ appearance. Weekend refresh cycle resolved the ambiguity: five independent legal-press primary sources (NicFab [1], banking.vision [2], EU Law Live [3], Modulos [4], lawandtechnology.eu [5]) converge on the same publication + EIF + deadline-shift set with matching regulation numbers. Fact — Stratum-material specifics: Stratum's HCM/ATS surface ships into Annex III standalone territory. The 2 August 2026 → 2 December 2027 shift is sixteen months of additional planning horizon for high-risk-obligation full application against Stratum's Cordova + Halcyon EU-nexus customer base — a materially different customer-conversation shape than the "six-day cliff" language every EU-nexus talking point has carried through eight weeks of daily briefs.
