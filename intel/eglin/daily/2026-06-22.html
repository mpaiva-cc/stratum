Lin signaled for the plaintiff. Workday is "an agent" under FEHA.

Per MLex's specialist coverage [4] and JDJournal's longer-form write-up [5], U.S. District Judge Rita Lin signaled on 2026-06-16 that Workday can be held liable under California's FEHA for discriminatory outcomes produced by its AI hiring tools even when the employers using those tools operate outside California. Fact: the legal classification Lin signaled is that Workday is not a "neutral software provider" but "an agent performing FEHA-regulated activities on the employer's behalf." Fact: this is a signal from the bench, not a final order; discovery continues and the ultimate outcome depends on what Workday's internal records reveal about how its algorithms were tested, validated, and monitored for discriminatory impact. Inference (labeled): the FEHA-cross-state-lines theory Eglin has tracked since the 5/27 tentative is now the operative judicial signal in the case.
