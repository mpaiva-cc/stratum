Why Ember is different

Not a faster megaphone. A relationship the candidate can trust.

The recruiting-CRM category spent a decade making the recruiter's outreach faster and almost none making it consented, deduplicated, or transparent to the candidate. We do not lead with "one graph" — that noun is contested. We lead with the three things our architecture does that a bolt-on CRM cannot retrofit.

01 · compliant by construction

Consent is an edge, not a checkbox.

A nurture campaign is a graph traversal that cannot cross an edge without valid consent for its purpose and jurisdiction. With the category shipping agentic outreach into EU AI Act high-risk rules and NYC Local Law 144, "compliant by construction" is the thing a General Counsel asks for by name.

02 · the pool that doesn't decay

Rediscovery over a resolved identity.

Incumbent pools are static snapshots that rot the moment they're built. Ember's pool is a live query over a graph whose identities are reconciled continuously across the customer's whole perimeter. The silver medalist who changed jobs is still the same node; the referral who is also a past applicant is one person, not two.

03 · the anti-ghosting product

Transparency the category forgot.

Recruiter refuses the black-box match score; Ember points that same explanation layer at the candidate. A person can see where they stand, what data is held, and revoke consent in one place. The ethics and the architecture are the same fact.

How it's priced

Priced on relationships kept, not seats filled.

The pure-play recruiting CRMs price per recruiter seat — which taxes you for growing the team doing the work. Stratum has never priced per seat, and Ember won't start. A platform fee plus a usage tier on the outcomes that matter — consented conversations and rediscovery-influenced hires — with the cheapest door being Ember as a module on a contract you already hold.

Module-first, on purpose. The Year-One goal is funnel completion and attach, not a standalone revenue headline. The standalone price exists to prove the motion travels — not to lead with it.

Recruiter hires the person in the pipeline. Ember is how you earn the right to the thousand who aren't in it yet — and never let them go cold.
