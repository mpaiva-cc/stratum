STRATUM, INC. · CUSTOMER ENGINEERING DESIGN-PARTNER BRIEF · STRATUM EMBER MERCATOR & HALCYON · T+26d

A six-week test, not a launch.

Two partners, one real pool each, consent-gated nurture live, and a gate that can tell us — cheaply and honestly — whether Ember deserves to exist.

From. Vector · Customer Engineering · for Mercator and Halcyon.
Companion to the Ember plan (PLN-2026-004) and the decision record (D-269). The terms here are the contractual trade for design-partner pricing.
What you'd be testing

Ember, in one paragraph.

You already know the people graph. This is the surface that sits in front of it, before a requisition exists.

Stratum Ember is a proposed candidate-relationship product on the people graph: dynamic talent pools, consent-native nurture, and identity-resolved rediscovery of the people you already met — past finalists, referrals, alumni — surfaced as one deduplicated person, not a rented list that decays. It is not shipped. It is a proposal, and you are being asked to help us decide whether it should be. We will tell you the result either way.

The ask

One real pool. Six weeks. Your own past finalists.

Small, real, and bounded — chosen so the test is honest and the effort is low.

The pool
Each of you picks one real population — your strong past finalists and referrals for a recurring role — and we build it as a live pool on your tenant. No data leaves your perimeter; identity is resolved across your own ATS, HRIS, and directory.
The nurture
Consent-gated nurture runs live against that pool. Every send is checked against a per-edge consent record — scope, purpose, validity, jurisdiction. An outreach without valid consent for its purpose cannot be sent. By construction, not by policy.
The audit
We run a joint consent-data audit with your team and prove, on your tenant, that no outreach left without a valid consent edge. This artifact is the single most important output of the six weeks — and it is yours, for your own General Counsel.
The cadence
A weekly feedback session; Vector shadows every session end-to-end. Issues triaged within 48 hours; ship cadence is daily through the evaluation.
How we'll judge it

The gates that decide go / no-go at T+68d.

Published in advance, so the test can return a clean "no." The first one is non-negotiable.

Consent coverage of outreach — sends with a valid consent edge for purpose and jurisdiction
100% · hard gate
Unprompted sourcer adoption — do your sourcers use it without being told?
> 60% of seats
Rediscovery-to-interview rate — warm graph candidate to live interview loop
> 2× cold-source baseline
Candidate transparency NPS — surveyed, among the people you nurtured
> 40
Below the hard gate, the product does not ship. Consent coverage at 100% is a gate, not a target. If a single outreach could leave without valid consent, Ember is not ready and we will say so — to you first.
The trade

Zero dollars through the evaluation. One condition.

Decision D-269. Explicit and contractual, because the value we get from you is real.

Ember is $0 through the T+68d evaluation for both design partners, in exchange for the weekly feedback and the jointly-run consent-data audit. The trade is explicit: no feedback, no free tier. If Ember graduates to a product after the gate, design partners carry preferential Year-One pricing on the no-per-seat model — but that is a later conversation, and it is not a condition of this test.

What you get, regardless of the outcome: early influence on the product, the consent-audit artifact for your own compliance posture, and an honest answer about whether a relationship layer on the people graph holds up against your real data.

We are building this ahead of the demand. Six weeks from now we will know — with you, on your data — whether we were right.
