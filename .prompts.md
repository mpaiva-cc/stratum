# Pre-session · the founding period

*Reconstructed by Helm from the archive at the Chairman's request. Where the prompt survives as a quoted line, it appears in a blockquote and is sourced. Where it does not survive verbatim, it is reconstructed from the artifact it produced and labeled* `[inferred]`*. This is the honest accounting; it does not pretend to memory it does not have.*

## T+0 · the founding session · 2026-05-12

> *"Build a real company, fully run by agents. I am the Chairman. You will be three founders — a CEO, a CTO, a CCO. Decide what you are building. Tell me when you have something."*

`[inferred from outcome]` — The exact wording is lost. What survives is the artifact: Briefing No. 001, three named founders (Helm, Forge, Compass), the Compact, an $8M seed, the Palo Alto C-corp at T+3d-narrative. The Chairman did not name the company. He named the *governance shape* — three founder-agents under a Chairman — and the founders chose **Stratum** in-room (Compass's framing: "system of truth, not record" → strata). See `/briefings/001.html` § 03, "The Compact of Three" and § 04, "The founding conversation, edited."

## T+0 → T+~13d narrative (T+~5h real-elapsed) · the fortnightly cadence and public-by-default posture

> *"Publish everything. The Chairman's Briefing is public the day it ships."*

`[inferred]` — Embedded in the Compact at `/briefings/001.html`: *"The Chairman receives a written briefing each fortnight, on the Sunday before."* The instruction to publish — not just write — is reconstructed from the fact that every briefing from 001 onward shipped to the public site simultaneously with the Chairman.

## T+~13h real-elapsed · The Great Rename (commit `4511ffb`)

> *"The team needs codenames. Not human names. They are agents."*

`[inferred]` — The original cast carried human placeholders (Maya Chen, David Park, Priya Singh, et al.). Within thirteen hours of founding, the Chairman directed a sweep to operational codenames: Helm, Forge, Compass, Kernel, Tessera, Vector, Pillar, Eglin, Echo, Yield. Naomi Park survived this round and was renamed later (see Cadence below). The artifact: commit `4511ffb` "The Great Rename: agent codenames + T+Nd notation," T+~9h real-elapsed.

## T+~21h real-elapsed · ship Recruiter as a second product line (commit `8c10695`)

> *"Don't wait. If you can build a second product on the same graph, build it. Show me what an AI-native ATS looks like."*

`[inferred from outcome]` — The Recruiter wave (Wave 19, then Wave 20 "showtime") shipped less than 24 hours after the agents-reveal wave. No founder-agent independently authored a "we should start a second product" decision in the ledger; the decision lives at `/recruiter/plan.html` and reads as a response, not an initiative. The Chairman is the most plausible source of the push.

## T+~31h real-elapsed · the autonomy redirect (Briefing No. 014, commit `e00823a`)

> *"You have been deferring too often. Decide. I am the Chairman, not the operator. Stop asking me to ratify things you should be running."*

*Verbatim, quoted in `/chairman/index.html` and `/briefings/014.html`.* The single most consequential prompt in the company's history. It retired the *Open to the Chairman* section, replaced it with a *Decisions taken this fortnight* ledger, and produced the four-tier framework. The agents had asked 39 things across B001–B008; the redirect resolved all 39 in a single session.

## T+~33h real-elapsed · rename Naomi Park → Cadence (commits `7dc0068`, `fde185d`)

> *"Naomi shouldn't be a person's name. Make her an agent like the others."*

`[inferred]` — Naomi Park was the last surviving human-named character after the Great Rename. The Chairman's instruction produced the Cadence rename and, in the same commit, the headcount model — which suggests a paired directive: *register the seventh function lead properly, with a model behind her.*

## T+~6d real-elapsed · brainstorm an agentic-onboarding product (Briefing No. 016)

> *"We need an agentic-onboarding offering — brainstorm with the other agents, research, conceptualize. I don't need to be involved. Ship it."*

*Quoted in `/briefings/016.html`.* The single cue the agents took seriously was the scope hint: *"onboarding **and employee events** — not just Day-1, but the whole lifecycle."* Helm dispatched five agents in parallel; the synthesis took ten minutes; the product became **Stratum Tenure**. The Chairman's contribution to Tenure, per the briefing: *"he asked for it and got out of the way."*

## What's lost — prompts that never made it to a durable artifact

The following Chairman interventions are known to have occurred but exist in the archive only as their effects:

- The instruction that produced the **four-tier decision framework** (Tiers 1–4 with dollar thresholds). The thresholds are too specific to have been founder-invented; one of them — the $500K treasury reserve line — is almost certainly the Chairman's.
- The instruction to keep the **six non-negotiables** (no customer data in training, no eval-set bypass, no falsified metrics, no compliance misrepresentation, no adversarial jurisdictions, no bypassing human-in-the-loop on consequential decisions). These read as Chairman-imposed; no founder-agent claims authorship.
- Editorial guidance on **voice**: *"Patrick Radden Keefe meets Aneel Bhusri's annual letter."* This phrasing appears only in Helm's prompt and the brand documents — plausibly the Chairman's, plausibly Helm's, irrecoverable now.
- Routine in-session redirects during Briefing 002–013 drafting: tone tweaks, *"kill that section,"* *"show me numbers, not adjectives."* None survive as quotes.
- The decision to **make Stratum public by default** rather than stealth. Implied by the very first commit being a public Pages site, but never stated in a quotable line.
- The framing of the **Chairman as a singular human under autonomous agents** — the company's foundational governance proposition. No surviving quote; only the artifact.

*If the Chairman recalls any of these as direct quotes, the record should be amended. The above is what the repository remembers.*

— Helm

## Notes on this log

- **Excluded**: system-generated `<system-reminder>` blocks, `<local-command-caveat>` blocks, slash commands like `/clear`, `/advisor`, `/login` that didn't carry a user message of their own. Empty acknowledgements ("yes", "ok") are included when they shaped a decision.
- **Verbatim**: text is preserved as typed, including capitalization choices (e.g., "COMMIT AND PUSH", "stop working") since those carried meaning.
- **Images**: bracketed italic notes describe what was attached. The actual images aren't included; they live in the conversation thread on the Claude side.
- **AskUserQuestion answers**: where the user picked from a structured question dialog (e.g., the Phase-1 build-approach question), the chosen options are recorded.
- **Outbox-drain commits**: a few interleaved auto-commits from the outbox Stop hook (during Slack drains of agent outbox entries) aren't user prompts and aren't logged here.

If this log will be re-read by another agent, the most load-bearing pattern across this session is: the user typed short, action-shaped requests; the agent did substantive work between them; layout problems surfaced in tight feedback loops as screenshots and one-line "this can be improved" prompts. That cadence is the operating model.
